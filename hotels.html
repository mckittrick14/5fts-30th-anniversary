Enid First Friday

A monthly community street festival held in the historic downtown square of Enid within walking distance to the GLō Best Western.

🗓️ When: First Friday of every month (Apr–Oct), 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM
📍 Where: Downtown Enid Square
🏛️ Host: Main Street Enid

What to Expect

  • Food & Drinks: Local food trucks, outdoor dining, and beverage vendors.
  • Shopping: Sidewalk sales and late-night openings from downtown boutiques and artisans.
  • Entertainment: Live music performances, family activities, and rotating monthly themes.

Nearby Dining Options

Downtown Enid offers several dining options within walking distance to the GLō Best Western.


    Enid Brewing Co. & Eatery (2 blocks)

    Local craft beer, burgers, and pub fare right on the downtown square.

    Callahan's Pub & Grille (3 blocks)

    A classic local spot for steaks, sandwiches, and relaxed evening drinks.

    Napoli's Italian Restaurant (2 blocks)

    Traditional pasta, pizza, and Italian favorites in a sit-down setting.
