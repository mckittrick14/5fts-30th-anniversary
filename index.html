5th Flying Training Squadron

30th Anniversary Reunion

Celebrating 30 Years of Excellence

Since its activation as an AETC Flying Training Squadron at Vance AFB, the Kittens have played a critical role in training the world’s finest military aviators. We are gathering current and former members, alumni, and families back in Enid to celebrate three decades of shared history, camaraderie, and aviation excellence.
